How the projection works

  • Each item counts by its weight in the syllabus. Weights total 100%.
  • Attendance & Participation is worth 5 points. Submitting the Claude for Excel task earns 1 of them; attendance and participation make up the other 4.
  • Excel Tutorial Modules are scored out of 100 on Blackboard and each count 2 points toward the course grade: a score of 50 or higher earns both points, 25–49 earns 1 point, and below 25 earns 0.
  • This page gives an estimate. Your official grade is the one posted by your instructor.
